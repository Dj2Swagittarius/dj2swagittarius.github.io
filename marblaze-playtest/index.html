Playtest Marblaze

Marblaze is a 3D endless marble runner with a Marble Brawl mode. There are two ways to help test it.

Paid Android playtest

$20 per hour of play, paid through PayPal. 5 spots.

  1. Apply with the form below.
  2. If you're picked, I'll send you the Android build and a short list of things to try.
  3. Play 3 sessions, then send one feedback form at the end.

Free web playtest

Open to anyone. No sign-up.

  1. Play in your browser, on phone or desktop.
  2. Send feedback here, or with "Send feedback" in the game's pause menu.
